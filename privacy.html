Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy covers That's Your Brain Uploader (the "Uploader") and this website. Both are operated by Jawad Ahmad, an individual creator ("I", "me"). Contact: jawadahmadjd@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

The Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using the Uploader you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the Uploader

The Uploader is a private tool. Its only user is me, the owner of the That's Your Brain YouTube channel, and it acts only on that channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from viewers or other YouTube users.

What information the Uploader accesses

  • Google sign-in authorization. When I sign in with Google, the Uploader receives OAuth access and refresh tokens that let it act on my channel with the permissions I grant: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and managing my channel's videos, playlists and comments (youtube.force-ssl).
  • My own video content and metadata. The video files I produce and their titles, descriptions, tags, category, audience settings, scheduled publish time, playlist and first comment.
  • Information returned by the API. The ID and processing status of each video the Uploader uploads, and the IDs of my channel and playlists, so it can record what was published and avoid uploading the same video twice.

The Uploader does not access other people's channels, viewer data, subscriber lists, analytics or private messages.

How the information is used

Only to upload, schedule and organise my own videos on my own channel. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The Uploader runs only on my own computer. The OAuth tokens and the upload records are stored locally on that computer; there is no server or database, and nothing is sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API. I do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

Neither the Uploader nor this website uses cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and neither places or reads information on your device. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Video IDs and upload records are kept only as long as they are needed to manage the channel's videos, and any data obtained from the YouTube API is refreshed or deleted within 30 days unless it is still needed for that purpose. Deleting the Uploader's local token file removes its access immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; when access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Questions and changes

Questions or deletion requests: jawadahmadjd@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
