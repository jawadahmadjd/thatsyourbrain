Calm, research-backed answers to why you do what you do.

That's Your Brain is a YouTube channel of short, illustrated explainers about everyday human psychology: why you suddenly notice something everywhere, why you can't ignore your own name, and the other small quirks that make you human. Every episode cites the research it is built on.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@ThatsYourBrain

That's Your Brain Uploader

That's Your Brain Uploader is a small private tool the channel's owner uses to publish the channel's own videos. After each episode is finished, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload the video to the That's Your Brain channel, set its title, description, tags and category, schedule its publish time, add it to a playlist, and post the channel's own first comment.

The tool is not offered to the public. It runs only on the owner's computer and only acts on the That's Your Brain channel, after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission. It uses YouTube API Services, and by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Privacy Policy for what it accesses and how to revoke access, and the Terms of Service.
